สไลด์ 12: Generic Answer — Examples
Q: For your project, provide an example of the system with Modular or Monolithic architecture. The example must include at least one rationale of why Modular or Monolithic Architecture has been chosen.
(From Gemini)
For our startup's new e-commerce platform, we chose a monolithic architecture. This system bundles the user interface, product catalogue, and payment processing into a single codebase. We selected this approach because it drastically simplifies initial deployment and testing. A monolith enables our small team to launch faster without managing the complex network overhead or distributed infrastructure required by modular microservices.
SCORE: C (C1) + F (C2) → D
(My own writing)
For our web-app project, chose Modular Architecture for the separation of codebase over Monolithic to improve maintainability and collaboration between Front-End and other components, such as database (via Supabase), which can be developed independently. This applies to both separation of Logic with Front-End and Logic with Database. Although complexity of the Modular system may be higher than Monolithic, we believe this trade-off is necessary to separate works in our teams & frontload the complexity of Database development during this time.
SCORE: A (C1) + A (C2)
